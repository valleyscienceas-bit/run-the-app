Mission Brief

What Are Balanced Forces?

In this lab, two teams tug on a box from opposite sides. Sometimes the box flies off — and sometimes it stays completely still even while both teams pull their hardest. Your mission: figure out exactly why. Start by forming a prediction.

Your Hypothesis

What do you think will happen to the box when both teams pull with the same force? Why?
