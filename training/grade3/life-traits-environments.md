# Life Cycles, Traits & Environments (Grade 3)

Living things have **life cycles** (birth, growth, reproduction). Offspring inherit **traits** from parents, and environments select which traits help survival.

## Ah-ha goal
Students link a trait to how it helps an organism survive in its habitat.

## Common misconceptions
- Traits appear randomly with no connection to parents.
- Environments "want" organisms to change overnight (vs. populations over time / inherited traits helping survival).
