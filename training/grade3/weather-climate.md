# Weather Patterns & Climate (Grade 3)

**Weather** is what the air is doing right now (temperature, wind, rain, clouds). **Climate** is the usual pattern of weather in a place over many years.

## Measuring weather
Scientists use instruments: thermometers (temperature), barometers (air pressure), anemometers (wind speed), rain gauges (precipitation).

## Patterns
Warm air can hold more moisture. Falling pressure often means a storm may be coming. Climate zones depend on latitude, sunlight, and nearness to oceans.

## Ah-ha goal
Students separate "today's weather" from "the climate where I live" and connect measurements to forecasts.

## Common misconceptions
- Climate and weather mean the same thing.
- Instruments "create" weather instead of measuring it.
