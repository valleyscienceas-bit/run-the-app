# Balanced Forces & Equilibrium (Grade 3)

When two forces push or pull on an object with the same strength in opposite directions, the **net force** is zero. The object is in **equilibrium**: it stays still or keeps moving at a steady speed in a straight line (**inertia**).

## Ah-ha goal
Students discover that equal opposite forces cancel — the cart does not speed up when ΣF = 0.

## Common misconceptions
- "The bigger arrow always wins" even when magnitudes match.
- "Moving means unbalanced forces" — constant velocity can still be balanced forces.
- Confusing force pairs with "winning" and "losing" sides.

## Vocabulary
Equilibrium, net force, vector magnitude, inertia, balanced forces, unbalanced forces.

## Classroom check
Ask: "If left thruster is 40 N and right is 40 N, what happens to speed? Why?"
