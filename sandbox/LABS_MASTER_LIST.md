# Valley Science — Baseline 20 Labs Master List
# Experiments are NOT changed yet. This is brainstorm / inventory only.

## Shared interactivity today (all 20)
- Two sliders (left + right) moved to thresholds
- Live telemetry numbers
- Step flow: intro → hypothesis → 6 lab steps → video → quiz
- Observation text boxes (Enter to submit)

## Differentiation ideas (for later — do not implement yet)
- Drag objects / magnets / animals instead of only sliders
- Tap-to-drop, draw-a-path, place barriers
- Multi-stage life-cycle buttons
- Touch/hold to apply force
- Match-color picker for camouflage
- Build-a-wall clickable bricks
- Compass / map tap for forecasts

### 3001 — Balanced Forces
- **About:** Robot on a frictionless track pulled by left/right rockets
- **Teaches:** Equal opposite forces cancel (net force = 0); Newton's First Law
- **Interactive now:** Two force sliders (N); watch robot accelerate or stay steady
- **Ideas later:** Drag rockets; thruster buttons; rope tug with teammates

### 3002 — Weather Patterns
- **About:** Temperature + air pressure make rain/snow/clear skies
- **Teaches:** Weather is what air is doing now; pressure drops often mean storms
- **Interactive now:** Temp (°C) + pressure (kPa) sliders
- **Ideas later:** Drag a cold/warm front across a city skyline; tap clouds

### 3003 — Weather Tools
- **About:** Measure wind and send a balloon high for data
- **Teaches:** Tools (anemometer, balloon) collect trustworthy measurements
- **Interactive now:** Wind speed + balloon height sliders
- **Ideas later:** Spin a cup anemometer with mouse; click launch balloon

### 3004 — Weather Forecasts
- **About:** Storm distance + pressure drop → forecast guess
- **Teaches:** Forecasts use patterns in multiple numbers
- **Interactive now:** Storm distance + pressure-change sliders
- **Ideas later:** Scrub a timeline; place sticky-note forecast on map

### 3005 — Climate Zones
- **About:** Latitude + sunlight strength → climate zone
- **Teaches:** Climate = usual weather over years; poles vs equator
- **Interactive now:** Latitude + sunlight sliders
- **Ideas later:** Spin a globe; drop a pin on Earth

### 3006 — Types of Climates
- **About:** Ocean distance + yearly rain → desert/tropical/etc.
- **Teaches:** Climate types from rain + ocean influence
- **Interactive now:** Ocean distance + rain sliders
- **Ideas later:** Paint rainfall on a continent map

### 3007 — Severe Weather
- **About:** Updraft + spin → tornado / flood risk
- **Teaches:** Severe weather hazards and why warnings matter
- **Interactive now:** Updraft + spin sliders
- **Ideas later:** Swipe to shear the storm; siren decision choice

### 3008 — Flood Protection
- **About:** Rising river vs wall budget
- **Teaches:** Engineering goals (criteria) vs limits (constraints)
- **Interactive now:** Flood height + budget sliders
- **Ideas later:** Stack sandbag bricks; spend coins on wall height

### 3009 — Life Cycles
- **About:** Food/water + baby rate keep a population going
- **Teaches:** Birth → grow → reproduce → death loop
- **Interactive now:** Nutrients + reproduce-rate sliders
- **Ideas later:** Tap life-cycle stages in order; care meter

### 3010 — Butterfly Metamorphosis
- **About:** Feed caterpillar + hormones → egg/larva/pupa/adult
- **Teaches:** Complete metamorphosis stages
- **Interactive now:** Food + hormone sliders
- **Ideas later:** Drag leaf to caterpillar; open chrysalis tap

### 3011 — Inherited Traits
- **About:** Mom + dad trait sliders → baby looks
- **Teaches:** Offspring inherit traits from parents
- **Interactive now:** Mom color % + dad color % sliders
- **Ideas later:** Pick parent faces; family tree drag-drop

### 3012 — Variation in a Species
- **About:** Gene mix + sample size → height spread in sunflowers
- **Teaches:** Same species can look different (variation)
- **Interactive now:** Mix % + plant-count sliders
- **Ideas later:** Plant seeds; measure with a virtual ruler

### 3013 — Environment Changes Traits
- **About:** Watering + soil nutrients change plant growth
- **Teaches:** Environment can change how traits show up
- **Interactive now:** Water days + nutrients sliders
- **Ideas later:** Water-can pour gesture; drought toggle

### 3014 — Learned vs Instinct
- **About:** Bell + training trials → learned response
- **Teaches:** Instinct (born with) vs learned (practice)
- **Interactive now:** Bell pitch + trial-count sliders
- **Ideas later:** Click bell; feed treat; trial counter game

### 3015 — Camouflage
- **About:** Match fur to ground so hawk catches fewer mice
- **Teaches:** Camouflage helps survival (adaptation)
- **Interactive now:** Fur color + ground color sliders
- **Ideas later:** Color picker / paint fur; hawk swoop mini-game

### 3016 — Working in Groups
- **About:** Pack size vs prey size → hunt success
- **Teaches:** Cooperation can help survival
- **Interactive now:** Wolves N + prey mass sliders
- **Ideas later:** Place wolves around prey; formation taps

### 3017 — Habitat Fit
- **About:** Temp + snow → which rabbit body plan thrives
- **Teaches:** Adaptations fit habitats (and fail elsewhere)
- **Interactive now:** Temperature + snow-depth sliders
- **Ideas later:** Swap Arctic/desert habitats with a portal door

### 3018 — Ecosystem Change
- **About:** Drain wetland vs plant willows → health
- **Teaches:** Disturbance hurts; restoration can help
- **Interactive now:** Drainage % + willow-count sliders
- **Ideas later:** Dig ditch / plant clickable willows

### 3019 — Gravity Pulls Down
- **About:** Latitude + mass; everything falls toward Earth's center
- **Teaches:** “Down” = toward center of Earth
- **Interactive now:** Latitude + mass sliders
- **Ideas later:** Spin Earth; tap-to-drop masses at poles/equator

### 3020 — Magnets Push & Pull
- **About:** Distance + strength → attract/repel without touching
- **Teaches:** Noncontact forces; closer = stronger
- **Interactive now:** Gap (mm) + strength sliders
- **Ideas later:** Drag magnets; flip poles N/S; maglev float track
